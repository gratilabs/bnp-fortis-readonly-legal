# Privacy Policy — Hermes BNP Paribas Fortis Read-Only Banking Connector

**Effective date:** 2026-07-04

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hermes BNP Paribas Fortis Read-Only Banking Connector processes bank account information when you connect your BNP Paribas Fortis account through an Open Banking provider such as Enable Banking.

## 1. Purpose

The application is used only to provide read-only personal finance visibility, including:

- viewing account balances;
- reading account transactions;
- producing summaries, alerts, and spending insights for the account owner.

The application does **not** initiate payments, transfers, standing orders, direct debits, or any other money movement.

## 2. Data processed

With your explicit consent, the application may process:

- account identifiers returned by the Open Banking provider;
- account names, currency, and masked account information;
- account balances;
- transaction dates, amounts, currencies, counterparties, references, and remittance information;
- Open Banking consent/session identifiers needed to retrieve the data.

The application does **not** collect or store your bank username, password, card PIN, itsme credentials, card-reader codes, or payment authorization credentials.

## 3. Source of data and consent

Bank data is accessed only after you authorize access through the Open Banking provider and BNP Paribas Fortis authentication flow. Authentication happens on the provider/bank pages, not inside the Hermes chat.

You may revoke access through the Open Banking provider or your bank's connected services/consent management interface.

## 4. Legal basis

The application processes account information based on your explicit consent for read-only account information access.

## 5. Storage and security

Data is stored locally on the account owner's Hermes machine in a local SQLite database. Provider API credentials and private keys are stored locally and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including local-only storage, least-privilege read-only access, and avoiding storage of bank login credentials.

## 6. Data sharing

The application does not sell bank data. Data is shared only as necessary to provide the service, for example:

- with the selected regulated Open Banking provider to retrieve account information;
- with the local Hermes assistant process to generate summaries requested by the account owner.

Bank data is not used for payment initiation.

## 7. Retention

Local transaction and balance data is retained only as long as needed for personal finance summaries and historical analysis. The account owner may request deletion of local stored data at any time.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data. Because this is a personal/local application, contact the account owner/operator for deletion or export requests.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator at the email address provided in the Open Banking application registration.

## 10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application, provider, or data processing changes.
